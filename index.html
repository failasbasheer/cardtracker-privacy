Card Tracker privacy policy

Developer: Trylas · Effective 14 October 2026

In short: Card Tracker reads your bank card alerts on your phone to add up your spending. Everything stays on your phone. The app has no internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere, and we never receive, collect or share any of it.

What the app reads

  • SMS messages (with your permission). Card Tracker reads incoming and existing SMS to find transaction alerts sent by banks. From each alert it extracts the amount, currency, merchant, the last four digits of the card, the transaction type and the available limit. Messages that are not card transaction alerts (for example personal messages, and one-time passwords or verification codes) are ignored and never stored.
  • Notifications from apps you choose (optional, with your permission). Some banks send alerts as app notifications instead of SMS. If you turn on notification access and pick those apps in the app's settings, Card Tracker reads notifications from those apps only, to find transaction alerts. Notifications from all other apps are ignored.
  • Your list of installed apps, only to show you the apps you can pick in that setting. It is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Information you enter, such as card nicknames, credit limits, due dates, categories, notes and transactions you add by hand.

Where your data is kept

All of the above is processed and stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Card Tracker does not have the Android internet permission. It has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party tracking, and it does not use any cloud backup of its data.

Sharing

We do not collect, sell or share your data with anyone. Data only leaves your phone if you choose to: for example when you use Export to spreadsheet or Share on an unrecognised message, which opens Android's share sheet so you pick where it goes. When sharing an unrecognised message, card numbers are hidden automatically.

Notifications

With your permission, the app shows notifications on your phone for new transactions and upcoming card payments. These are created on the device.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual transactions or cards in the app, or everything at once in Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device. You can withdraw SMS or notification access at any time in Android settings.

Children

Card Tracker is meant for adults managing their own payment cards and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
